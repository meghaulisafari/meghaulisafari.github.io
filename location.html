Location

Where we are

Hotel Meghauli Safari is in Meghauli, in the Chitwan district of Nepal, on the western side of Chitwan National Park. The park's western entrance is close enough to reach on foot or by a short drive, and the Rapti and Narayani riversides are a short trip away.
