Stay by the jungle, for less than you expect

Hotel Meghauli Safari sits in Meghauli, at the western side of Chitwan National Park. Clean, quiet rooms with breakfast and hot water around the clock — at prices below the hotels around us. Nepali travellers and visitors from abroad are equally at home here.

  • Breakfast included

Why guests choose us

Good value, without cutting the things that matter

Meghauli has no shortage of places to sleep. What we offer is a genuinely lower price than the hotels nearby, without giving up the things you actually notice on a trip — a clean private bathroom, hot water whenever you want it, breakfast before an early safari, and somebody at reception at any hour.

Most of our guests are here for one reason: Chitwan National Park is on the doorstep. We are glad to help you plan around it, whether you arrive with a full itinerary or none at all.
