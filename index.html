Your Gateway to Wild Nepal

Hotel Meghauli Safari is more than just a place to stay—it's your basecamp for exploring the incredible biodiversity of Chitwan National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Located on the tranquil banks of the Rapti River in Meghauli, we offer the perfect blend of comfort and adventure.

Wake up to the calls of exotic birds, embark on thrilling rhino safaris through ancient forests, and witness the majesty of Bengal tigers, gharial crocodiles, and over 500 species of birds. Our experienced guides ensure safe, educational, and unforgettable encounters with nature.

After your adventures, retreat to our comfortable rooms with modern amenities, savor authentic Nepali cuisine at our riverside restaurant, and share stories around the evening bonfire. Whether you're a wildlife photographer, nature enthusiast, or seeking a peaceful escape, Hotel Meghauli Safari is your perfect sanctuary.

How to Reach

✈️

By Air

Fly to Bharatpur Airport (25 km away). We offer complimentary airport pickup - just let us know your arrival time when booking.

🚌

By Bus

Regular tourist buses run from Kathmandu (5-6 hours) and Pokhara (4-5 hours) to Sauraha/Meghauli. We can arrange pickup from bus stops.

🚗

By Car

Drive along the Narayanghat-Mugling Highway. Follow signs to Meghauli. Free parking available on-site.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking

What is the best time to visit Chitwan National Park?

The best time to visit is October to March when the weather is pleasant and wildlife sightings are most frequent. October-November offers lush greenery after monsoon, while February-March provides excellent visibility with shorter grass.

What activities are included in the stay?

Complimentary breakfast is included with all rooms. Safari activities, jungle treks, bird watching tours, canoe rides, and cultural shows are available at additional cost. We can help arrange customized packages.

How far is the hotel from Bharatpur?

We're located 25Kms away from Bharatpur Airport and about 4Kms away from Meghauli Airport.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in. Cancellations within 48 hours are subject to a one-night charge. We recommend travel insurance for flexibility.

Is WiFi available?

Yes! Free high-speed WiFi is available throughout the hotel, including all rooms and common areas.

Are children welcome?

Absolutely! We welcome families. Children under 6 stay free when sharing with parents. We can provide extra beds for older children at nominal charges. Safari activities are suitable for children aged 5+.
