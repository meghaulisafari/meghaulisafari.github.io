Experiences

Safaris and river trips

Chitwan National Park is the reason most people come to Meghauli. We arrange safaris and boat rides for our guests through local operators, so you can settle everything at reception rather than hunting for a guide after you arrive.

Tell us what interests you when you send your booking request, or ask at reception once you are here — the desk is staffed 24 hours.

How this works

We arrange these activities rather than operate them ourselves. Safaris inside Chitwan National Park require an authorised guide by law, and we book yours through operators who hold that authorisation.

Prices above are per person. Where a permit or a guide is charged separately, we have said so on the card rather than leaving it to be discovered at the gate.
